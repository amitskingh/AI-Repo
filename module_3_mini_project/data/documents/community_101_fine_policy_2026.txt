Community 101 Fine Policy

Version: 2026
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

General Policy

The community may impose fines when residents violate the governing documents.

Violation Process

A resident may receive a notice describing the violation and the applicable rule.

Fine Structure

The amount of a fine depends on the type and severity of the violation.

Maximum Fine

The maximum fine for a violation is $1,000.

Appeals

Residents may appeal a fine according to the community's governing documents.
